Cookie Policy

How Meridian Capital Partners uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 1 January 2025

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how the site is used. We also use similar technologies such as local storage to remember your settings and account state.

2. Cookies we use

CategoryPurposeConsent
Strictly necessarySecurity, session management, load balancing and remembering your cookie choice.Not required
FunctionalRemembering your language, region and display preferences.Required
AnalyticsUnderstanding how visitors use the platform so we can improve it.Required
MarketingMeasuring the effectiveness of campaigns and showing relevant content.Required

3. Local storage

In addition to cookies, the Meridian Capital Partners platform uses your browser's local storage to keep your account, transactions and preferences available between visits. This data stays on your device and is not shared with third parties by us.

4. Managing cookies

When you first visit the platform, you can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's cookies and local storage, or through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the platform from working.

5. Third-party cookies

Some third-party services we use, such as analytics providers, may set their own cookies. These are governed by the respective providers' privacy policies. We only allow such cookies where you have consented.

6. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at privacy@meridiancapital.co.uk.
