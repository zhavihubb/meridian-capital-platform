4. Trading fees

Online trades in equities, ETFs and funds are commission-free. A foreign-exchange conversion fee of up to 0.5% may apply where you trade in a currency other than sterling. Third-party fund charges are disclosed in each product's key information document.

5. Loan fees

A 5% arrangement fee applies to approved loans and is disclosed before you sign. Representative APRs range from 3.2% to 16% depending on the product and your circumstances. Early-repayment charges may apply to certain products.
