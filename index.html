Everything You Need to Know

Everything you need to know before opening your Meridian Capital Partners account.

Meridian Capital Partners operates in alignment with UK investment rules and works within FCA regulatory expectations. Client onboarding includes full KYC/AML verification, client funds are held in segregated accounts at tier-1 UK banks, and eligible assets are covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS).

You can open an account with no minimum balance, and start investing from £100. Fractional investing lets you buy portions of expensive instruments — for example, a slice of the FTSE 100 or S&P 500 index — so you never need thousands to begin.

Client money is held in segregated accounts, completely separate from company operating funds. Data is protected with 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption and two-factor authentication. In the unlikely event of platform failure, eligible assets remain yours and are covered by the FSCS up to £85,000 per investor.

Our fee structure is fully transparent: no account opening fees, no inactivity fees, and no hidden charges. You pay a clear custody fee on assets, transaction fees on trades, and loan arrangement fees that are always shown before you sign. See our Fee Schedule for the complete breakdown.
