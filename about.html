Our Story

Built on UK Financial Standards

Meridian Capital Partners was founded on a simple conviction: that British investors deserve a platform that combines the transparency and rigour of traditional UK financial institutions with the speed and accessibility of modern technology. From our offices in Canary Wharf, we serve investors across the United Kingdom and beyond.

Our platform follows AML/KYC compliance, investor protection standards, and transparent fee structures aligned with UK regulatory frameworks. We hold client funds in segregated accounts at tier-1 UK banks, and we publish our fees openly — no hidden charges, ever.
